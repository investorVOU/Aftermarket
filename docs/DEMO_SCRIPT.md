# Human-recorded demo

Record the actual connected testnet game. No video or social post has been
published by the agent. Keep keys, .env, session storage and operator consoles off
screen. Do not present local development inventory as on-chain ownership.

## Main sequence

1. Start with **Play as guest / demo account**. Show the island immediately, with
   NEAR TESTNET visible and no seed phrase, wallet popup or gas prompt.
2. Gather three timber, visit Oak & Iron, craft furniture and sell it. Show the
   inventory and credit changes. The rule is three timber + $2 labour → one item;
   selling gives $50 non-redeemable game credits.
3. Open the exchange. Point to a stock-based quote and its reasoning. Start the
   **Seven days. One minute.** preview. Keep the price history, trade counter and
   treasury visible. Production, purchasing and the daily lease use the normal
   simulation engine.
4. When the yard closes, show the unpaid bill and spend your own game credits on
   recovery. Show arrears falling, the reopening event and resumed trading. If the
   town differs because it has already progressed, explain the actual state; do
   not replace it with a staged frontend number.
5. Open the confirmed item inspector for yourself, then Timber Supply. Show their
   distinct owner IDs and final contract balances. Open a real mint/burn receipt
   and its NEP-245 owner/item log. The game remains useful without opening it.

Optional longer segment: unlock First Trade, fulfil a workshop contract and buy
the Trading Post. Stock it and set a competitive price. Show the bound business
licence in the inspector. Do not call a previous player's business your new guest's
business; separate saved islands are explicit.

## Narration facts

- The economy is the game loop: supply, pricing, production, cash and operating
  costs create choices. NPCs cannot buy unlimited stock or spend money they lack.
- The server validates transactions and persists the world; offline catch-up uses
  the same rules. NEAR records confirmed items, game credits and business licences.
- Guest/email authentication maps to server-managed ledger identities. This is
  managed custody, not MPC or independently funded native accounts for every NPC.
- Credits are non-redeemable game units, not native NEAR or real money. All chain
  activity shown is testnet, with operator-sponsored gas/storage.
- Existing Claude v0.6 world/art/local mechanics are disclosed baseline; the
  reviewed diff and new testnet evidence identify the integration work.
